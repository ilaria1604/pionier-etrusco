Privacy and cookies

What we do with the data of people who visit this site and write to us.

Who handles the data

The data controller is Coreal di Eleuteri M. & C. s.n.c., which runs Camping Pionier Etrusco: Via Vulsinia 2, 01014 Marina di Montalto di Castro (VT), Italy, VAT no. 01513620565, REA 90814. For any question about your data, write to info@pionieretrusco.com or call +39 0766 802807.

What data we use and why

Requests from the form, emails and phone calls

  • Data: name, email, phone, dates of stay, accommodation, number of people and whatever you choose to write to us.
  • Why: to reply to you and prepare your stay.
  • Legal basis: your request and pre-contractual measures (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • How long: up to 24 months from the last contact. If you book, the necessary data is kept for as long as tax and accounting rules require.

Without the required form fields we cannot answer your request. Form data is not used for newsletters or advertising.

Visit statistics, only if you accept

  • Data: pages viewed, length of visit, type of device, approximate location and a random browser identifier.
  • Why: to understand which pages are useful and improve the site, using Google Analytics. Advertising features are turned off.
  • Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you can withdraw at any time.
  • How long: up to 14 months.

Technical browsing data

  • Data: IP address, type of browser, date and time of visits.
  • Why: to run the site and protect it from abuse.
  • Legal basis: our legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • How long: for as long as strictly necessary for these purposes.

Who can see the data

The data is seen by the people who work at the campsite. We do not sell it to anyone. Some suppliers help us and process it on our behalf:

  • Google for email and for the record of requests (Gmail and Google Sheets) and for statistics (Google Analytics), the latter only if you accept.
  • The company that hosts the site, which processes the technical browsing data.

Some of these suppliers are based in the United States. In those cases the transfer takes place under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or the standard contractual clauses approved by the European Commission. We may disclose data to the authorities only when the law requires it.

Cookie

We do not use advertising or profiling cookies. Only these items can be stored on your device:

  • cp-consenso (this site, browser storage): remembers your cookie choice for 6 months. It is technical and does not require consent.
  • _ga e _ga_* (Google Analytics): tell visitors apart in the statistics, for up to 2 years. They are stored only if you accept.

If you refuse, the site works just the same.

Your rights

You can ask us to show you your data, correct it, delete it or restrict its use, to give it to you in a readable format, and you can object to its processing. Just write to info@pionieretrusco.com. You can also lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority (Garante per la protezione dei dati personali).

Changes

If we change this page, we update the date at the top.
