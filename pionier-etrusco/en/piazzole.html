Pitches

Pitches in the heart of the pine wood, in the shade of the pines, for motorhomes, caravans, tents and rooftop tents. And a camper stop area for short stays, where your dog is welcome too.

What you will find

In the shade, 100 metres from the sea.

Our pitches are in the heart of the pine wood. The shade of the pines keeps off the summer heat and gives you a green space close to nature.

There are areas for motorhomes, tents and caravans. Tell us how you travel and we will suggest the right one.

Good to know

Before you arrive.

  • In high season the minimum stay is 7 nights
  • Departure by 12 noon
  • A minimum of 2 people is charged per pitch
  • ID documents for everyone in your party are required on arrival
  • ID wristbands are mandatory
  • Only one sleeping unit per pitch
  • Dogs are not allowed in the camping area
  • Monthly and full-season rates: see the prices

Camper stop area

An area designed for short stays, with all the comforts of the campsite. The price includes electricity, hot showers, toilets, and water and waste at the on-site motorhome service point.

We are pet friendly. Small and medium-sized dogs are welcome in the camper stop area, always on a lead. They can come with you to the restaurant, the pizzeria and the bar. The pool, the playground and the camping area are off limits.
