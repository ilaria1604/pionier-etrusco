What's included

Camping under the pines, in a wooden bungalow.

Every bungalow has its own veranda, for breakfast in the shade and dinner in the cool of the evening.

Each has a double room, an equipped kitchen, a bathroom with shower and air conditioning for the hottest days. The two-bedroom bungalows also have a second room: with a bunk bed in the 28 sqm, with single beds in the Superior 48 sqm.
